# Privacy Policy for DiamondScript

**Last Updated:** February 10, 2026

DiamondScript ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our mobile application.

---

## Information We Collect

### 1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

DiamondScript stores the following data **locally on your device only** (not on our servers):

- **Practice Plans:** Practice sessions you generate, including age group, intensity, drill selections, and station layouts
- **Practice History:** Records of past practice sessions you've saved
- **Starred Drills:** Drills you've marked as favorites
- **Custom Drills:** Drills you've created yourself
- **App Preferences:** Your last used settings (age group, experience level, etc.)
- **Purchase Status:** Whether you've purchased DiamondScript Pro (one-time purchase)

**This data never leaves your device** and is stored using Android's secure local storage (AsyncStorage).

### 2. Crash Reports and Error Tracking

We use **Sentry** to collect anonymized crash reports and error logs to help us improve app stability. This includes:

- Device type and operating system version
- App version and build number
- Error messages and stack traces
- Anonymized crash reports

**We do NOT collect:**
- Your name, email address, or any personal identification
- Location data
- Contact information
- Photos or media files
- Any personally identifiable information (PII)

### 3. Purchase Information

When you make a one-time purchase for DiamondScript Pro:

- **Payment processing** is handled entirely by Google Play Store
- We only receive a confirmation that the purchase was successful
- We **DO NOT** collect, store, or have access to your payment details (credit card, billing address, etc.)
- All payment data is managed securely by Google

---

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

1. **Provide Core Functionality:**
   - Generate practice plans based on your input
   - Save and restore your practice history
   - Remember your preferences and favorites

2. **Improve App Quality:**
   - Debug and fix crashes
   - Identify and resolve technical issues
   - Improve app performance and stability

3. **Verify Purchase Status:**
   - Enable Pro features for users who have purchased DiamondScript Pro
   - Restore purchases across devices

## Data Sharing and Third Parties

### We DO NOT sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

**Third-party services we use:**

1. **Google Play Store** (payment processing only)
   - Purpose: Process one-time Pro purchase
   - Privacy Policy: [Google Play Privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

2. **Sentry** (crash reporting only)
   - Purpose: Anonymized error tracking to improve app stability
   - Privacy Policy: [Sentry Privacy](https://sentry.io/privacy/)
   - Data collected: Anonymized crash reports, device type, OS version
   - **No personal data is sent to Sentry**

---

## Data Storage and Security

- **Local Storage:** All practice data, drills, and history are stored locally on your device using industry-standard encryption
- **No Cloud Storage:** We do not sync your data to the cloud or store it on our servers
- **Data Retention:** Data remains on your device until you delete the app or clear app data
- **Backup:** Your data may be included in device backups (managed by your device settings)

---

## Your Data Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

### 1. **Access Your Data**
All data is stored locally on your device. You can view it within the app at any time.

### 2. **Delete Your Data**
You can delete your data at any time by:
- Deleting individual practice sessions or drills within the app
- Clearing app data in your device settings
- Uninstalling the app (permanently removes all data)

### 3. **Export Your Data**
Currently, the app does not provide a data export feature. All data remains local to your device.

### 4. **Opt-Out of Crash Reporting**
Crash reporting helps us fix bugs and improve the app. It is enabled by default but contains no personal information.
